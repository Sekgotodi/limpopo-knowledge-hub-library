About Limpopo Knowledge Hub Library

Strategic Goals & Core Values

Limpopo Knowledge Hub Library is a fictional academic library established to support students, educators, researchers, and community members in their pursuit of knowledge through ethical and accessible information literacy.

Our Strategic Goals

  • Lifelong Learning: Promote recreational and scholarly reading and lifelong educational empowerment.
  • Multi-Format Access: Provide robust access to print collections and scholarly electronic information resources.
  • Information Literacy: Support undergraduate and postgraduate students with research skills and source evaluation.
  • Responsible Usage: Encourage the responsible, legal, and copyright-compliant use of information.
  • Digital Modernization: Improve access to educational knowledge through state-of-the-art digital web technologies.
Guided by international academic library standards and South African higher education priorities.

Our Core Values

Accessibility

Rooted in our commitment to inclusive scholarship and student empowerment.

Expert Guidelines for Searching Academic Databases

1. Keyword Focus Use concise, targeted terminology and boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) to filter relevance.
2. Temporal Filters Restrict publications to the last 5 years for fast-moving technical or medical subjects.
3. Author Credibility Verify peer-review status, author affiliations, citations, and DOI registry numbers.
4. Citation Etiquette Always document source identifiers to generate accurate references in Harvard, APA, or IEEE.
5. Full-Text Check Abstract listings might link to paywalls; check institutional repositories for open preprints.
