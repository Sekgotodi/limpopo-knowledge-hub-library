About Limpopo Knowledge Hub Library

Limpopo Knowledge Hub Library is a fictional academic library established to support students, educators, researchers, and community members in their pursuit of knowledge through print collections, electronic databases, and dedicated research literacy assistance.

Our Strategic Goals

  • Promote lifelong learning: Cultivate a vibrant culture of reading and intellectual curiosity throughout the academic community.

  • Support academic research: Guide students and faculty with research methodologies and deep information literacy.

  • Digital technology integration: Improve accessibility through modern open-access digital repositories and remote tools.

Tips for Effective Database Research

Advice from Reference Librarian Kabelo Ramokgopa

Target Specific Keywords

Avoid typing entire essay questions. Use Boolean operators like AND, OR, and quotation marks for exact key terms.

Apply Search Filters

Refine your query by publication date range (e.g., last 5 years), peer-review certification, language, or geographical scope.

Verify Source Credibility

Check author affiliations, methodology rigor, peer-review status, and journal standing before citing in assignments.

Check Open Licensing

Remember that a search listing in an index does not always mean full-text access is unpaywalled. Look for "Open Access" flags.

Accurate Referencing

Ensure you capture complete metadata (DOIs, authors, publication titles) to build correct bibliographies in Harvard or APA format.

Policy & Governance

Library Rules and Regulations

To maintain a welcoming, safe, and productive learning environment for all scholars, users are expected to strictly observe the following library regulations.

1–5

General Patron Conduct

1. Noise Control Maintain silence or speak quietly in designated communal zones. Strict silence is mandatory in quiet study wings.
2. Mutual Respect Respect library staff and fellow patrons at all times. Harassment, verbal hostility, or disruption will not be tolerated.
3. Cleanliness Keep the library clean. Dispose of all waste, papers, and rubbish in the designated recycling bins.
4. Property Care Do not deface, highlight, dog-ear, or damage books, furniture, desktop terminals, or library fixtures.
5. Staff Directives Follow all instructions provided by library staff, security personnel, and posted official advisory notices.
6–10

Borrowing & Circulation Regulations

6. Valid Membership Card Present a valid student or staff library membership card when borrowing materials. Cards are strictly non-transferable.
7. Timely Returns Return borrowed materials on or before the specified due date to prevent overdue fines and account blocks.
8. Renewal Procedures Renew books prior to the due date via the online portal or circulation desk, provided no recall holds exist on the item.
9. Lost or Damaged Materials Promptly report lost, mislaid, or water-damaged materials to circulation staff for immediate replacement invoicing.
10. Account Security Do not lend your library card or digital account credentials to another student or external individual.
11–14

Technology & Computing Rules

11. Responsible IT Usage Use library workstations, Wi-Fi networks, and public printers exclusively for academic, research, and educational purposes.
12. Prohibited Content Do not access, stream, download, or distribute illegal, sexually explicit, harmful, or inappropriate content on library devices.
13. Copyright & IP Rights Strictly adhere to intellectual property laws. Do not systematically scrape or reproduce entire commercial copyright textbooks.
14. System Configurations Do not install unauthorized software, alter system proxy settings, or unplug computer hardware cables.
15–18

Study Space Etiquette

15. Mobile Phones Keep cellular phones on complete silent mode. All phone calls must be taken outside library entrance halls.
16. Seat Reservation Do not reserve study desks with books or personal items for long periods. Unattended belongings after 30 minutes may be cleared.
17. Food & Beverages Only spill-proof water bottles are permitted in general study areas. Eating and open beverages are strictly restricted to the outdoor cafeteria.
18. Personal Belongings Keep laptops, wallets, and bags secure at all times. The library accepts no liability for items left unattended.
19–20

Privacy, Security & Compliance

19. Respect User Privacy Respect the confidentiality and privacy of other scholars. Do not record patrons or photograph screens without explicit consent.
20. Incident Reporting Immediately report any suspicious activity, unattended packages, safety hazards, or damaged electrical equipment to staff or security.
Enforcement Policy: Failure to follow library rules may result in formal warnings, fines, or restricted access to library facilities and electronic systems in accordance with institutional disciplinary codes.
